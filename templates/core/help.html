MMS HELP CENTER

How can MMS help you today?

These instructions are written for everyday use. What you can see and change depends on your role.

My profile

  1. Open Record Sale.
  2. Choose Customer Sale or General / Walk-in Sale.
  3. For a customer sale, start typing the customer ID, name, phone or address and select the correct result.
  4. Choose Retail or Wholesale pricing, then add products and whole-case quantities. MMS loads the matching product price automatically.
  5. Check the total and submit.

Salesmen can correct their own transactions until the configured daily cutoff. After that, contact the supervisor.

Every zone has one shared daily dispatch number in YYMMDDNN format. Individual transactions then use SALE-[dispatch]-00001, 00002, and so on for that zone/day.

Use the Customers page to search instantly by customer ID, name, phone or address. Salesmen use existing assigned customers; supervisors, monitors and authorized management users create new customers when needed. New customer IDs are generated automatically in the format CUST-[Zone]00001. Click anywhere on a customer row to open the full record.

Filters apply automatically. Use the Cases and Cash buttons to switch the same analysis between physical sales volume and monetary value. Charts, KPI cards, rankings and comparisons update with the selected date range, salesman, zone and sale type.

Users with export permission can download a structured Excel workbook containing KPI analysis, daily trends, salesman, product, zone and customer performance, sale mix and transaction detail. For System Admin, Manager and Sales Clerk users, Excel also includes a Daily Sales Report matrix with Disp#, supervisor, salesman, one column per product, cases, cash and transaction totals. CSV remains available for raw-data workflows.

Never share your password. Use the eye button only when nobody can see your screen. The password checklist shows required rules in red/green as you type.

MMS may warn when a password is similar to the username, but this similarity warning does not block the password if all mandatory password rules are satisfied. Permissions are enforced on the server, important changes are audited, and inactive sessions are signed out according to the system setting.

Use Field Monitoring to record which salesman you monitored, how many shops you visited, customer and shopkeeper comments, company chiller presence, observations and follow-up needs. Your customer and salesman choices are limited to your assigned zone. You can also review your own monitoring performance and history.
{% endif %} {% if role_code == 'SUPERVISOR' or role_code == 'MANAGER' or role_code == 'CLERK' or role_code == 'ADMIN' %}

Use Reports & Analytics to compare salesmen, products, customers and zones by both cases sold and cash value. Supervisors see their team scope. Managers and sales clerks see the broader market scope allowed by their role. Unit-price overrides are available to these higher operational roles and are recorded when the sale is saved.
{% endif %} {% if role_code == 'ADMIN' or user.is_superuser %}

The System Administrator manages users, roles, zones, global settings, the company logo, Backup & Restore and the Audit Log. Core System Administrator permissions are protected so the administrator cannot accidentally remove the access required to administer or recover MMS.

Use Backup & Restore to download a restorable .mmsbackup package. Before any restore, MMS creates a server-side pre-restore safety snapshot. For disaster recovery when the web interface is unavailable, the same backup and restore operations are also available through Django management commands documented in the README.
